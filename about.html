Our Vision

To see couples living as one flesh in Christ, united in purpose, grounded in Kingdom principles, and equipped to build marriages that are strong, purposeful, and life-giving, becoming places of refuge, strength, and lasting Kingdom legacy.

Our Mission

What we are here to offer

At One Flesh Community (OFC), we exist to build a Christ-centered community where singles and couples are equipped to grow in Christ, build healthy relationships, strengthen marriages, and establish homes that reflect God's design and influence generations. OFC is a community where singles prepare, individuals grow, couples strengthen, families flourish, and generations are impacted, all while pursuing God's purposes together with Jesus Christ at the center.

Leadership Profiles

Meet the team

Mr. Kofi Edu-Ansah

President, First Among Equals
  • Founding Member of One Flesh Community (OFC) and passionate about building strong, purposeful, and Christ-centered marriages and families.
  • Husband and Father, married to Mrs. Robertha Edu-Ansah, with whom he is raising two children.
  • Clinical Psychologist with over eight years of experience at Korle-Bu Teaching Hospital, spanning marriage and couples counseling, sexual and relational counseling, psychotherapy, and adherence counseling.
  • Highly Qualified Professional, holding an MPhil and BSc in Psychology from the University of Ghana, Legon, and an MA in Conflict, Peace and Security from the Kofi Annan International Peacekeeping Training Centre.

Mr. Joseph Quarshie

Chief Operating Officer, Operations & Strategy
  • Founding Member of One Flesh Community (OFC) and Nee Joe Ministries, building faith-centered communities through music, teaching, and creative platforms.
  • Christian leader and community builder, passionate about strengthening relationships and creating meaningful communities.
  • Technology professional experienced in AI training, data annotation, and model evaluation, bringing both music and technology to serve the ministry.

Mrs. Dorcas Quarshie

Chief People Officer, People, Community & Care
  • Mother of 3, deeply passionate about people and committed to serving, inspiring, and making a meaningful difference wherever she finds herself.
  • Professional teacher and Education Professional by training, passionate about learning, growth, and positively impacting lives.
  • AI Trainer, bringing the same care for growth and development into her technology work.
  • Vice President of Nee Joe Ministries, actively involved in outreaches to young people, mentoring, and empowering the next generation.

Mrs. Robertha Edu-Ansah

Chief Financial Officer, Finance & Admin
  • Wife and mother, married to her best friend Mr. Edu-Ansah for 8 years and counting, and blessed with two children.
  • Founding Member of One Flesh Community (OFC), passionate about building a Christ-centered community that strengthens marriages, families, and individuals.
  • AI Trainer and Earth Science Researcher currently pursuing a PhD at the University of Ghana, with a background in geophysics and scientific research.
  • Community Builder and Initiator, having started book clubs that encourage women to read, grow, learn, and invest intentionally in themselves.
