What Is OFC

A community built for real, lasting oneness

OFC exists for people at every stage, single, courting, engaged, or married, who want their relationships shaped by Scripture rather than culture or convenience. Every applicant is personally reviewed, and next steps include a short onboarding conversation with one of our founding couples.

I

Grow in Christ

Every teaching, conversation, and gathering flows from a shared faith in Jesus Christ.

II

Strengthen Marriages

Whether preparing for marriage or strengthening one already underway, every stage is treated with intention.

III

Connect, Celebrate & Belong

Real friendships, mentorship, and accountability with people walking the same road.
