Community Values

Our Community Values

OFC is a Christ-centered community built on Scripture, respect, growth, and genuine support.

Community Guidelines

The following guidelines shape how we live out our values together, day to day.

I

Foundational Commitment

We follow Jesus Christ, submit to Scripture, and pursue Kingdom marriage as a lived way of life.

II

Confidentiality & Trust

Protect what is shared here. No screenshots, forwarding, gossip, or sharing struggles without permission.

III

Conduct & Communication

Communicate with respect, avoid unsolicited counsel, resolve disagreements privately, and maintain boundaries.

IV

Engagement & Participation

Respond to posts, empathize, celebrate wins, and contribute without trying to solve others' situations.

V

Advice & Support Boundaries

This is a discipleship community, not a substitute for professional counseling.

VI

Leaving & Removal

You may leave at any time. Leadership may privately remove members for serious or repeated violations.
